When you decode a classmate, you see the three intel reports from their card. Your job is one decision: do you trust this agent, or are they a traitor? How do you tell? Compare. A loyal agent's reports match every other loyal agent from the same role. A double agent's planted report won't. So if you're not sure, find another card from that same role on the Padlet and decode it too. The game puts the two side by side and tells you which reports are different. A difference means one of them is lying, but not which one, so you may need a third agent, or the Archive, to decide. Then choose Trust or Traitor and seal your verdict. It can't be changed. Your verdict goes into the reply the game writes for you as a SEAL code, so nobody reading the Padlet can tell what you decided. Only the agent you judged can open it.
