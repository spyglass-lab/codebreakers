When you decode a classmate, you see the three intel reports from their card. Your job is one decision: do you trust this agent, or are they a traitor? You don't need to check anything or decode extra cards. Go with your gut, like real intelligence officers who often had no way to check their sources: does this sound right, and do you trust this person? If you happen to have decoded two agents from the same role anyway, the game gives you a bonus clue and shows which reports are different. The Archive is there if you want background, but you don't need it. Then choose Trust or Traitor and seal your verdict. It can't be changed. Your verdict goes into the reply the game writes for you as a SEAL code, so nobody reading the Padlet can tell what you decided. Only the agent you judged can open it.
