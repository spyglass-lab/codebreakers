Trust or traitor

Judge a classmate

1

Read their three reports. Is one of them a lie?

2

Go with your gut, or check the Archive if you want to. Does it sound right? Do you trust them?

3

Click Trust or Traitor. Your verdict is sealed inside your reply. For your first 2 classmates, the reply is copied too.

Bonus clue: if you’ve already decoded two agents from the same role, the game shows which reports differ. The Archive button opens declassified files where you can check facts.

When you decode a classmate, you see the three intel reports from their card. Your job is one decision: do you trust this agent, or are they a traitor? You don't have to check anything or decode extra cards. You can go with your gut, like real intelligence officers who often had no way to check their sources: does this sound right, and do you trust this person? Or, if you want to check, click Read the Archive: it opens declassified files on each role's part of the war, and you can compare the reports with them. If you happen to have decoded two agents from the same role anyway, the game gives you a bonus clue and shows which reports are different. Then click Trust or Traitor to seal your verdict. It can't be changed. Your verdict goes into the reply the game writes for you as a SEAL code, so nobody reading the Padlet can tell what you decided. Only the agent you judged can open it. For your first two classmates, that click also copies your reply.
