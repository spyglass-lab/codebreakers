Decode a classmate

  • Copy their whole Padlet post and paste it into the Decoder board.
  • You see their role, their ending and every choice they made.
  • Their pieces of the messages unlock on your board.
  • Click Trust or Traitor: the game writes your reply and copies it for you.

The message pieces are blacked out on these slides. You’ll see the real words in the game.

Here is what you see when you decode a classmate. Copy their whole Padlet post, paste it into the Decoder board and press Decode. The game finds their Signal ID for you, so there's nothing to type. You get their file: their role, their ending, their score, and the choice they made in every round. Their pieces of each secret message unlock on your board too. The pieces are blacked out on this slide so nothing is given away. Below the file, the game writes a reply for you that starts with DECODED and their Signal ID, followed by your proof code, and your sealed verdict once you choose Trust or Traitor. The proof code is made from your own Signal ID and theirs, and it goes in your submission too, so your teacher can find your reply on the Padlet. Finish your own game first, or the reply has no proof code. When you click Trust or Traitor, the reply is copied for you, with a question about a round where you two chose differently. Paste it under their card on the Padlet. If you want a different round or question, open See or change your reply.
