Here's the best part. Every reply a classmate posts under your card carries a SEAL code. That's their secret verdict on you: trust or traitor. Nobody reading the Padlet can tell what it says, but you can. Come back to your card later, copy the replies, and paste them into Who trusted you? on your Decoder board. If you were loyal, you see how many classmates trusted you. If you were a double agent, you see how many you fooled. It works even if your classmates play at different times, because the seals wait on the Padlet until you come back. It's just for you and doesn't change your grade. As for your own verdicts on other people: you find out if you were right once you've decoded all six messages.
