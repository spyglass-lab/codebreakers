On the Padlet

1

Post your card in your role’s column. Click Copy card and text in the game, then paste twice: card first, then the text.

2

Decode classmates from the other four roles. Copy their whole post, paste it into the game’s Decoder board, then click Trust or Traitor.

3

Reply to 2 classmates. Paste the reply under their card. The click already copied it, with your proof code. Your teacher checks that your replies are really there.

Sign in to Padlet with your school account before you post.

Now the Padlet. Sign in with your school account. Step one: post your card in the column for your role. In the game, one button copies your card image and your post text together. On the Padlet, click plus in your role's column and paste: your card appears. Then click in the text box and paste again: your post text appears. Step two: find cards from the other four roles, copy each whole post and paste it into the Decoder board in the game, then click Trust or Traitor. Step three: reply to two of the classmates you decode. For your first two, that click copies the reply the game wrote for you, starting with DECODED, their ID, your proof code and your sealed verdict, comparing your choices in one round. Just paste it. After two replies you're done replying, though more are welcome. You still decode all four other roles for the messages, and the Decoder board tells you which roles you still need. The same proof codes are in your submission, so your teacher can search the Padlet and see that every reply was really posted.
