Sign in to Padlet with your school account before you post.

Now the Padlet. Sign in with your school account. Step one: post your card in the column for your role, with the card image and the post text from the game. Step two: find cards from the other four roles, copy each whole post and paste it into the Decoder board in the game, then click Trust or Traitor. Step three: reply to every card you decode. That click copies the reply the game wrote for you, starting with DECODED, their ID, your proof code and your sealed verdict, with a question for them. Just paste it. The same proof codes are in your submission, so your teacher can search the Padlet and see that every reply was really posted. Answer the questions people ask you, too.
